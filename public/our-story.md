# Our Story

Plain-text version of the web page “Our Story: Peter Parizhsky, founder | Tech-Savvies” at https://tech-savvies.com/our-story/.

Summary: Tech-Savvies was founded in 2020 by Peter Parizhsky. In 2026 it returns as an affordable website creation, repair, and online presence optimization business.

Tech-Savvies was founded in 2020 by Peter Parizhsky as a project to help older adults with technology and computer troubleshooting. In 2023, he paused the project to focus on school. In 2026, Tech-Savvies returns as an affordable website creation, repair, and online presence optimization business. Peter continues to run the business and manage every project that comes through it himself. Tech-Savvies is the trading name of Peter Parizhsky.
