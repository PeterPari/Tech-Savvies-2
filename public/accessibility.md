# Accessibility

Plain-text version of the web page “Accessibility | Tech-Savvies” at https://tech-savvies.com/accessibility/.

Summary: How Tech-Savvies makes this website accessible, what we’ve tested, what we know is still limited and how to tell us about a barrier.

Last updated September 29, 2026

## Our goal

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, zoom or high-contrast settings. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, version 2.2, Level AA.

## What we’ve checked

- We tested every page with an automated checker, axe-core, on screens from 320 to 1280 pixels wide, with the menu open and with the contact form’s error messages showing.
- We went through every page with a keyboard alone and checked that you can always see where you are.
- We checked every page at 200% zoom and with wider text spacing, so nothing is cut off and you don’t have to scroll sideways.
- We checked the site with reduced motion turned on and in high-contrast mode.

## Known limitations

None known as of September 29, 2026. So far we’ve tested in Chrome-based browsers, not yet with a screen reader.

## Tell us about a barrier

If part of this site is hard to use, or you can’t get the information you need, email [info@tech-savvies.com](mailto:info@tech-savvies.com?subject=Accessibility) (the subject “Accessibility” is filled in for you). Tell us which page and what went wrong. We typically reply within 1 business day.

If you need a page’s information another way, every page also has a plain-text version: add .md to the page’s name, for example [tech-savvies.com/privacy.md](https://tech-savvies.com/privacy.md) for the Privacy Policy or [tech-savvies.com/index.md](https://tech-savvies.com/index.md) for the home page. [tech-savvies.com/llms.txt](https://tech-savvies.com/llms.txt) lists them all.

## What this site relies on

This site relies on HTML and CSS. JavaScript is optional: without it, the menu shows as a list of links and the contact form still works.
