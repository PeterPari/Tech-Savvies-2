Accessibility

Last updated September 29, 2026

Our goal

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, zoom or high-contrast settings. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, version 2.2, Level AA.

What we’ve checked

  • We tested every page with an automated checker, axe-core, on screens from 320 to 1280 pixels wide, with the menu open and with the contact form’s error messages showing.
  • We went through every page with a keyboard alone and checked that you can always see where you are.
  • We checked every page at 200% zoom and with wider text spacing, so nothing is cut off and you don’t have to scroll sideways.
  • We checked the site with reduced motion turned on and in high-contrast mode.

Known limitations

None known as of September 29, 2026. So far we’ve tested in Chrome-based browsers, not yet with a screen reader.

Tell us about a barrier

If part of this site is hard to use, or you can’t get the information you need, email info@tech-savvies.com (the subject “Accessibility” is filled in for you). Tell us which page and what went wrong. We typically reply within 1 business day.

If you need a page’s information another way, each page also has a plain-text Markdown version: add .md to the page’s name, for example tech-savvies.com/privacy.md.

What this site relies on

This site relies on HTML and CSS. JavaScript is optional: without it, the menu shows as a list of links and the contact form still works.
